# Welcome — let's get your booking page live

This is everything you need to do to be ready for inbound bookings. Six
short steps. Should take under ten minutes start to finish. No
engineering knowledge required.

If you get stuck on any step, just reply to whoever sent you this email
— a real person reads every reply.

---

## 1. Open your invite link

You'll have an email from us with a subject line that includes "Your
booking page is ready." Inside is a link with your name in it — open it.

That takes you to your personal booking page.

> **Don't see the email?** Check spam, then ping us. We'll re-send.

---

## 2. Set your password

You're already signed in via the link, but before you do anything else,
set a real password so you can log back in later:

1. Click your initials in the top-right.
2. Choose **Settings** → **Account** → **Change password**.
3. Use something you'll actually remember (or, better, drop it into your
   password manager).

> We sent you a temporary password in the welcome email. You can keep it
> if you like — but most people overwrite it with their own.

---

## 3. Connect your calendar

This is the most important step. Without it, the booking page won't
know when you're free.

1. **Settings** → **Calendars**.
2. Click **Connect** next to whichever calendar you live in — Google,
   Outlook, Apple iCloud, etc.
3. Approve the permissions when your calendar provider asks. We need
   read access (to know when you're busy) and write access (to put
   confirmed bookings on your calendar).

> **Two calendars?** Connect both. You can pick which one new bookings
> get written to under **Settings → Calendars → Add to calendar**.

---

## 4. Confirm your timezone

1. **Settings** → **General** → **Timezone**.
2. Make sure it matches the timezone you actually work in.

This is the timezone prospects see your availability in. If you travel,
check it occasionally — the page won't auto-detect.

---

## 5. Confirm your working hours

We pre-set you to **Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.** in the
timezone you confirmed in step 4. If those hours are right for you,
**skip this step**.

If they need tweaking:

1. **Availability** in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the schedule named "Working Hours."
3. Drag the day rows to widen, narrow, or block off days.
4. **Save** at the bottom right.

> A few defaults you don't need to touch:
>
> - Each meeting is **30 minutes**.
> - There's a **15-minute buffer** before and after every meeting (so
>   you're never back-to-back).
> - Prospects need to book **at least 4 hours ahead**.
> - Prospects can book **up to 60 days out**.
> - **Up to 5 meetings per day** — past that, the page shows you as
>   booked.
>
> If those defaults aren't right for your business, tell us and we'll
> change them centrally.

---

## 6. Test it once, then you're done

Open your booking page in a private/incognito window:

> `http://localhost:3000/<your-slug>/30min`
>
> (We sent you the exact link in the welcome email.)

You should see your name, the meeting length, and time slots that match
the working hours you just set. Pick one and book it as if you were a
real prospect — use your own email so you get the confirmation. Then
delete it from your calendar.

If everything looks right: **you're live**. The link in your welcome
email is what you give out from now on — drop it in your email signature,
LinkedIn bio, anywhere prospects might find you.

---

## What happens next

Every time a prospect books, reschedules, or cancels:

- **Your calendar updates immediately.**
- **You get a confirmation email** from your booking page.
- **We get notified too** — so we can follow up on warm leads as they
  come in. (You don't have to do anything for this; it just happens.)

If your booking page is ever down for any reason, prospects who hit your
link see a polite "the booking page is temporarily unavailable" message
with a direct mailto: link to you, so they can still reach out.

That's it. Welcome aboard.
